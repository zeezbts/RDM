History of This College

Raja Doraisingam Government Arts College in Sivagangai, Tamil Nadu, was founded on August 11, 1947, by Raja Srimath Muthu Vijaya Regunatha Gowri Vallabha Doraisingam Shanmuga Raja to provide education to rural students. Originally private and affiliated with the University of Madras, it was taken over by the Government of Tamil Nadu in 1981 and is now affiliated with Alagappa University.

  • Founder: Established by Raja Doraisingam Shanmuga Raja in memory of his father.
  • Establishment: Founded on August 11, 1947, immediately following India's independence.
  • Transition: Became a government institution in 1981.
  • Location: Located on a sprawling 216.65-acre campus in Sivagangai.
  • Academic Evolution: The Department of History was established in June 1970. The institution offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Arts, Science, and Commerce.
  • Accreditation: The college was accredited with a 'B+' grade (first cycle) and later 'B' grade (second cycle) by NAAC.

The college fosters comprehensive character-building alongside academics. Through active bodies like the National Cadet Corps (NCC), students cultivate leadership, disciplined living, and patriotic ideals, serving as a pillar of community engagement within the region.
